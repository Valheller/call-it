A big update to **Call it.**: 110 updates since v1.0.0.

### Windows
Download **Call-it.exe** below and run it. Nothing to install, and your progress saves between launches.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app isn't code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Any other device
Play at [valheller.github.io/call-it](https://valheller.github.io/call-it/), or open [`index.html`](https://github.com/Valheller/call-it/blob/main/index.html) in a modern browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it runs full screen like an app.

## What's new since v1.0.0

### New places to go
- **The Back Room** is now a proper shop for players with bad Karma: cheap cursed Rare Charms, loaded coins and easy money.
- **The Big Spender** opens for anyone holding over $100: Legendary and Fabled Charms at eye-watering prices, all carrying the new **Inflation** curse, plus the **Spirit Select** pack of downside-free Spirits. It keeps showing up until you buy something there.
- **The Prize Counter** and **Prize Booth**: spend the Tickets you earn on special Charms.
- Stage title cards introduce each floor, its boss and any weather.

### New ways to play
- **Challenge Rolls** unlock after your first win, plus the **Speedrun**, **Locked** and other new Rolls.
- **Betting**: on the first toss of a wager, bet your money double-or-nothing on 4+ hits. Bets are capped by a table limit that rises each stage, and the House takes 25% of any money you hold over $100.
- **Boss rerolls**: once per stage from the boss card, plus the Inside Man charm, Rune of Exile and Boss Dossier packs.
- **Rival rematches**, a wandering ghost that turns up if you dawdle, and new Charms (Skee-Ball, Mimic, Understudy, Paradox, Rewind Tape, Curtain Call, Bank Teller and more), Spirits (R Key) and Runes.
- **Retry** from the Game Over screen with the same roll and stake.

### Music and sound
- Every shop and detour has its own theme: the Shop (bossa nova), Bazaar, Chapel, Back Room (after-hours jazz), Big Spender (big-band swing), Prize Counter (chiptune) and Spirit Select.
- Stage music adds a layer each floor instead of speeding up, and Stage 8 plays a full 32-bar piece. Endless builds toward its final bosses.
- Phones: no more ticking or popping, and the sound fades out cleanly when you leave the app.
- A Sound Test with every track, a stage picker and Glitched audio.

### Collection and records
- Collection tabs for Bosses, Rivals and Upgrades, with Luck and Karma shown on items. Every Upgrade has its own icon.
- A Records screen with stats (including your most flipped coins), run history and a numbered unlock grid.
- Run Info shows toss scores, your Upgrades and How to Play. Save codes let you move your progress between devices.

### Phones and polish
- Fits iPhone screens properly, including when launched from the home screen.
- On phones, hold a coin or Charm to read it, and drag to buy, sell, use or rearrange. No more Save Image menu when you hold on game art.
- Legendary Charms glow gold and Fabled Charms shimmer, in the shop and in packs.
- A bigger "Called it." victory card on computers, coin count under your purse, missed coins stay greyed, and many smaller fixes.
